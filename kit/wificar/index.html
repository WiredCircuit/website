Welcome & Introduction

Welcome to the Wired Circuit Wi-Fi Car Robotics Kit! This kit is designed for kids and beginners to explore the world of robotics using Wi-Fi-enabled microcontrollers. You’ll learn about electronics, programming, and how to build and control your own car wirelessly!

Box Contents

  • NodeMCU (ESP8266)
  • Motor Driver (L298N / Custom)
  • Aluminum Chassis
  • 2 DC Motors
  • Wheels (x2)
  • Battery holder and switch
  • USB cable
  • Jumper wires
  • Mounting hardware (screws, nuts, etc.)

Assembly Instructions

  1. Mount the motors to the chassis.
  2. Fix the motor driver on the top plate.
  3. Attach the wheels to the motor shafts.
  4. Connect NodeMCU to the motor driver as per the diagram.
  5. Insert the battery and switch into the holder.

Programming Guide

  • Install Arduino IDE and NodeMCU board package.
  • Use USB to connect the board to your computer.
  • Upload the sample code provided in the resources section.
  • Make sure to install required libraries (ESP8266WiFi, etc.).

Wi-Fi Setup

Power the NodeMCU and connect to the provided Wi-Fi hotspot or input your home Wi-Fi credentials in the code. Access the car control interface through the displayed IP in your browser.

How It Works

The NodeMCU receives commands over Wi-Fi and sends signals to the motor driver to control the motors. The motor driver directs power to the wheels depending on the inputs from NodeMCU.

Troubleshooting

  • Not connecting to Wi-Fi – Check credentials.
  • Motors not working – Verify power and connections.
  • Upload failed – Recheck port and driver settings.

Add-Ons & Upgrades

  • Add line following sensors
  • Bluetooth control with HC-05 module
  • Ultrasonic sensor for obstacle avoidance
