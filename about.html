About Wired Circuit

Sparking Curiosity, Building Futures in Nepal.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for technology and education, Wired Circuit began as a small idea to make learning electronics and robotics accessible and exciting for everyone in Nepal. We saw a gap in hands-on STEM learning opportunities and were driven to create high-quality, engaging kits that could empower students, hobbyists, and aspiring innovators. From humble beginnings, we've grown into a dedicated team committed to fostering a culture of creativity and problem-solving.

Our journey has been fueled by the belief that practical experience is key to understanding complex concepts. We are proud to design and develop our kits locally, ensuring they are relevant, affordable, and supported by comprehensive resources.

Our Mission

Wired Circuit's mission is to ignite curiosity and empower the next generation of innovators in Nepal through accessible, hands-on STEM education. We strive to:

  • Provide high-quality, affordable robotics and electronics kits.
  • Develop engaging learning materials and resources.
  • Foster a community of learners, makers, and educators.
  • Promote critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills.
  • Support schools and institutions in integrating practical STEM learning.

We believe that by making technology education fun and approachable, we can help build a brighter, more innovative future for Nepal.
